Privacy Policy — Email Agent

Last updated: March 1, 2026

Thank you for using Email Agent. This privacy policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and what we never do with it.

What the App Does

Email Agent is an AI-powered email management tool that helps you triage, unsubscribe from, and organize your inbox. It connects to your email accounts, classifies messages using artificial intelligence, and presents them for your review and action.

Data We Access

Your Email

Email Agent connects to your email accounts (Gmail, Yahoo Mail) using industry-standard OAuth 2.0 authentication. The app reads email messages in order to classify them and present them for your review.

Your email content is processed entirely on your device. It is never transmitted to our servers, never stored in the cloud, and never shared with any third party by us.

Account Credentials

OAuth tokens used to access your email are stored in your device's secure encrypted storage (iOS Keychain). We never see your email password. You can revoke access at any time through your Google or Yahoo account settings.

AI Processing

By default, email classification is performed using an on-device AI model (Gemma). Your email content never leaves your device during this process.

If you choose to use a third-party AI provider (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI ChatGPT, or Google Gemini) by entering your own API key, email content will be sent to that provider according to their respective privacy policies. This is entirely optional and controlled by you.

App Settings and Patterns

The app stores your preferences, trust/block lists, and learned email patterns locally on your device in a private database. This data is not transmitted anywhere.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your email content
  • We do not collect your email address or account credentials
  • We do not collect analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports
  • We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • We do not have servers that receive any of your personal data

In-App Purchases

If you purchase Email Agent Pro, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple's App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Children's Privacy

Email Agent is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
